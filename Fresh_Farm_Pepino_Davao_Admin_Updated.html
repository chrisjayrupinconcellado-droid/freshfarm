🌿 Executive Venture Summary

Fresh Farm Pepino Davao is a local agricultural Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) venture focused on delivering fresh, crispy, locally harvested pepino directly from the farm to customers in Davao City.


By reducing unnecessary middlemen, the business aims to preserve produce freshness, provide affordable pricing, and create a convenient digital ordering experience for customers.
